2. What SalaryMan Is

SalaryMan is a personal tool that shows, in real time, roughly how much you have earned so far this month. It works entirely from figures you enter yourself: your monthly pay, your working hours, your fixed costs, and any days you mark as leave.

It is a way of seeing your work in motion. That is all it is.

3. What SalaryMan Is Not

This section matters more than the rest. Please read it.

• SalaryMan is not payroll software. It has no connection to your employer, your HR system, or your bank.

• The amounts shown are estimates calculated from what you typed. They are not your actual pay.

• Your real pay is determined solely by your employer and your employment contract. It will differ from what this app shows, because the app does not account for taxes, social insurance, overtime, allowances, deductions, or rounding.

• SalaryMan does not provide financial, tax, accounting, legal, or employment advice.

• Do not rely on SalaryMan for any decision that matters. Check your payslip.
