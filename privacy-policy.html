SalaryMan

Privacy Policy

SalaryMan collects nothing. What you enter stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Last updated August 23, 2026

1. What We Collect

Nothing.

SalaryMan has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no servers, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

The one exception is if you choose to email us. Section 8 explains that.

2. What You Enter

To calculate your earnings, SalaryMan asks you for your monthly pay and currency, your working hours, your fixed monthly costs, the days you mark as leave, any one-off bonus, and any part-time shifts you schedule. Part-time work also has a name you type in yourself, which may be an employer’s name or anything else you like — it is treated exactly like every other figure below.

It is stored in the app's own storage on your device, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — a copy is kept in your own iCloud account so that it survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Sections 3 and 4 describe both of these.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, SalaryMan stores a copy of your settings in Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This copy lives in your own iCloud account, under your own Apple Account. It is not sent to us, and we cannot read it: we operate no servers and have no way to access your iCloud data.

Its only purpose is to give your settings back to you if you delete and reinstall the app, or set up a new device. If you are not signed in to iCloud, this does not happen and everything stays on the one device.

You can turn this off at any time in the iOS Settings app, under your Apple Account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

4. Apple Watch

If you use SalaryMan on Apple Watch, your settings are sent from your iPhone to your paired Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. That transfer happens directly between your two devices. It does not pass through us, and it does not pass through any third party.

5. Network Access

SalaryMan makes one kind of network request, and only one: at most once a day it asks Apple’s public App Store lookup service whether a newer version of SalaryMan has been released. The request contains the app’s bundle identifier and your App Store country. The reply contains a version number. Nothing else is sent, and nothing about you is sent.

We never contact a server operated by us — we do not run one. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

The only other data that leaves your device is the iCloud copy described in section 3, which is handled entirely by Apple’s own system frameworks, and the transfer to your Apple Watch described in section 4.

Your figures — pay, hours, shifts, wishes, everything you type — are never transmitted anywhere by us.

6. Face ID

Before erasing all of your data, SalaryMan asks iOS to confirm it is you — with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. This is the only place the app asks for it.

The check is performed entirely by iOS. SalaryMan never sees your face, your fingerprint, or your passcode; it is told only whether the check succeeded. If it does not succeed, nothing is erased.

7. Calendar

Sync To Calendar is off unless you turn it on. If you do, SalaryMan asks iOS for calendar access and creates its own calendar, named “SalaryMan”, on your device. Your Arbeit shifts are written there, and kept in step when you add, change, or remove one.

SalaryMan writes only its own events and never reads the rest of your calendar. iOS grants full calendar access rather than add-only because updating and removing an event we created is not possible with add-only access — without it, changing a shift would leave a duplicate behind.

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The events live on your device and, if you use iCloud for calendars, in your own iCloud account — the same as any event you create yourself.

Turning the switch off deletes every event SalaryMan created and removes its calendar. Events you made yourself are never touched.

8. If You Email Us

The app has a Feedback button. Tapping it opens your own email app with our address, a subject line, and the app and system version already filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it.

If you do send it, we receive whatever you wrote and the email address you sent it from — the same as any email. We use it only to answer you and to fix what you reported. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass it to anyone else.

Your figures are never attached. If you want us to see a number, you have to type it in yourself.

9. Deleting Your Data

Deleting SalaryMan from your device deletes everything stored on that device.

If iCloud is enabled, the copy in your iCloud account remains — which is what allows your settings to return when you reinstall.

To remove everything at once, use Clear All Data at the bottom of Settings inside the app. It erases the data on this device and the copy in your iCloud account, and it asks you to confirm with Face ID first. This cannot be undone.

You can also delete SalaryMan’s data from iCloud in the iOS Settings app, under your Apple Account. Because we hold no copy of anything, there is nothing for you to request from us.

10. Children

SalaryMan is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and the date at the top will change.
