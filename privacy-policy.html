SalaryMan

Privacy Policy

SalaryMan collects nothing. What you enter stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Last updated August 6, 2026

1. What We Collect

Nothing.

SalaryMan has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no servers, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

2. What You Enter

To calculate your earnings, SalaryMan asks you for your monthly pay and currency, your working hours, your fixed monthly costs, the days you mark as leave, any one-off bonus, and any part-time shifts you schedule.

It is stored in the app's own storage on your device, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — a copy is kept in your own iCloud account so that it survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Sections 3 and 4 describe both of these.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, SalaryMan stores a copy of your settings in Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This copy lives in your own iCloud account, under your own Apple Account. It is not sent to us, and we cannot read it: we operate no servers and have no way to access your iCloud data.

Its only purpose is to give your settings back to you if you delete and reinstall the app, or set up a new device. If you are not signed in to iCloud, this does not happen and everything stays on the one device.

You can turn this off at any time in the iOS Settings app, under your Apple Account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

4. Apple Watch

If you use SalaryMan on Apple Watch, your settings are sent from your iPhone to your paired Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. That transfer happens directly between your two devices. It does not pass through us, and it does not pass through any third party.

5. Network Access

SalaryMan contains no networking code of its own. It never contacts a server operated by us or by anyone else. The only data that leaves your device is the iCloud copy described in section 3, which is handled entirely by Apple's own system frameworks.

6. Deleting Your Data

Deleting SalaryMan from your device deletes everything stored on that device.

If iCloud is enabled, the copy in your iCloud account remains, which is what allows your settings to return when you reinstall. To remove that copy as well, delete SalaryMan’s data from iCloud in the iOS Settings app, under your Apple Account. Because we hold no copy of anything, there is nothing for you to request from us.

7. Children

SalaryMan is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and the date at the top will change.
