SalaryMan — User Agreement

SalaryMan estimates your earnings from numbers you type in yourself. It is not payroll software, and the amounts it shows are not your actual pay. The news summaries and market prices on its Home page are general information, not advice.

Last updated October 5, 2026

By using SalaryMan you agree to this agreement. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. What SalaryMan Is

SalaryMan is a personal tool that shows, in real time, roughly how much you have earned so far this month. It works entirely from figures you enter yourself: your monthly pay, your working hours, your fixed costs, and any days you mark as leave.

It is a way of seeing your work in motion.

Its Home page also shows a short briefing of world news and a board of market prices. Sections 5 and 6 cover those.

3. What SalaryMan Is Not

This section matters more than the rest. Please read it.

• SalaryMan is not payroll software. It has no connection to your employer, your HR system, or your bank.

• The amounts shown are estimates calculated from what you typed. They are not your actual pay.

• Your real pay is determined solely by your employer and your employment contract. It will differ from what this app shows, because the app does not account for taxes, social insurance, overtime, allowances, deductions, or rounding.

• SalaryMan does not provide financial, tax, accounting, legal, or employment advice.

• Do not rely on SalaryMan for any decision that matters. Check your payslip.

4. Your Figures

You are responsible for the numbers you enter and for keeping them current. SalaryMan has no way to verify them, and it will faithfully calculate from whatever you give it.

5. News and Market Information

No human editor checks them. They can be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Follow the link to the original reporting before relying on anything you read there.

Market prices and charts are shown for general information only. They may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate, and they may stop being available at any time. They are not suitable for trading.

Nothing in SalaryMan is investment, financial, or trading advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell anything. SalaryMan does not know what you own, and it never relates the news or the markets to your own money.

The names of news organisations, companies, indices, and other marks belong to their respective owners. SalaryMan is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any of them.

We may change, suspend, or remove the news and market features at any time.
