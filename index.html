One number that is always moving in your favour.

Type in your take-home pay and your working hours. From then on the only thing you do with SalaryMan is watch.

01

Every second counts

Enter your pay and your hours once. The number climbs every second you are at work and stands still when you are not. Lunch breaks included — your pay doesn't stop for lunch either.

02

Your Free Income Day

Tell it your monthly fixed costs. SalaryMan shows the exact moment rent and bills are covered — and from that second on, everything you earn is yours. It celebrates with you.

03

World Brief

A short daily briefing: a few world and money stories, two sentences each — what happened and why it matters for your wallet — with links to the original reporting, in 11 languages.

04

Markets and charts

A board of indices, currencies, commodities and well-known stocks. Add your own, tap a tile for the chart and a few published ratios. Plain figures: no ratings, no recommendations.

05

Part-time work

Add a second job with its own hourly rate, schedule the shifts and get a reminder before each one. They count toward the same monthly total and tick in real time too.

06

Wish List

Put a price on the things you are saving for. SalaryMan tells you the day you get there — calculated from what you actually earn, after living costs.

07

Wealth Simulator

Choose a target passive income and an expected return; see the assets you would need and how long it takes at your pace. A calculation to play with — not financial advice.

08

Trajectory

Every month settles into a ledger you can scroll back through — pay, bonuses and shifts, month by month, from the day you started.

SalaryMan Privacy Policy User Agreement Support

SalaryMan is a personal tool, not payroll software. It estimates from the figures you type in yourself; the amounts it shows are not your real pay. News summaries are written by AI from public headlines, and prices may be delayed — general information, not investment advice.
